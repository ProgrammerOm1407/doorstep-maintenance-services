FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about technician visits, warranties, and rates.

What are your diagnostic fee policies?
+

Our diagnostic evaluation fee is completely waived if you proceed with the recommended repair service. If you choose to only receive the inspection diagnostics without resolving the repair, a flat $39 consultation fee is charged to cover technician travel and evaluation time.

How does the 90-day warranty policy work?
+

If the exact component we repaired or replaced fails within 90 days of service, we will return and fix it with zero diagnostic, parts, or labor fees. An official digitally signed warranty receipt is emailed instantly upon service completion.

Are your technicians certified and insured?
+

Yes. Every technician undergoes background verification check-ups, holds verified trade licensing certifications, and carries comprehensive liability insurance coverage up to $1,000,000 to protect your household space.

Which payment options do you support?
+

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), secure online wallet links, checks, or cash payments. The service technician is equipped with a digital terminal for instant processing upon invoice generation.
